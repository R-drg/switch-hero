SWITCH HERO - INSTALL
=====================

You need a Switch that runs homebrew (Atmosphere + the Homebrew Menu).


1. COPY TO THE SD CARD
----------------------

Copy the whole "switch-hero" folder content into the "switch" folder at
the root of your SD card. When you're done it should look like this:

    SD card
    └── switch
            ├── switch-hero.nro
            └── songs
                └── First-Light      (demo song)


2. LAUNCH
---------

Open the Homebrew Menu and start Switch Hero.

For full memory, open the Homebrew Menu by holding R while starting any
game, rather than from the Album. This is recommended for big songs and
needed if the download keyboard won't open.


3. ADD SONGS
------------

Either:

  - Press + on the song list to download charts from Chorus Encore
    (enchor.us). The console must be online. Y searches, A downloads,
    minus goes back.

  - Or copy extracted Clone Hero song folders into
    sd:/switch/switch-hero/songs/
    Each folder needs notes.chart or notes.mid plus its audio
    (.opus, .ogg, .mp3 or .wav) and usually a song.ini.


4. FIRST TIME SETUP
-------------------

Go to Settings (Y on the song list):

  - Controller mode: press-to-hit (Joy-Con / Pro Controller), strum,
    or Wii guitar.
  - Audio / input offset: press A and tap along to the clicks.
  - Visual offset: press A and tap as the notes cross the line.
    Do the audio offset first.

Hold minus for two seconds on any screen to get back to the song list.
