SWITCH HERO - INSTALL
=====================

You need a Switch that runs homebrew (Atmosphere + the Homebrew Menu).


1. COPY TO THE SD CARD
----------------------

Copy the whole "switch-hero" folder into the "switch" folder at the root
of your SD card. When you're done it should look like this:

    SD card
    └── switch
        └── switch-hero
            ├── switch-hero.nro
            └── songs
                └── First-Light      (demo song)


2. LAUNCH
---------

Open the Homebrew Menu and start Switch Hero.

For full memory, open the Homebrew Menu by holding R while starting any
game, rather than from the Album. This is recommended for big songs and
needed if the download keyboard won't open.


3. ADD SONGS
------------

Either:

  - Choose DOWNLOAD SONGS on the title menu (or press + on the song
    list) to get charts from Chorus Encore (enchor.us). The console must
    be online. Y searches, A downloads, B goes back.

  - Or copy extracted Clone Hero song folders into
    sd:/switch/switch-hero/songs/
    Each folder needs notes.chart or notes.mid plus its audio
    (.opus, .ogg, .mp3 or .wav) and usually a song.ini.


4. FIRST TIME SETUP
-------------------

Open OPTIONS on the title menu (or Y on the song list):

  - Gameplay > Controller mode: press-to-hit (Joy-Con / Pro Controller),
    strum, or Wii guitar.
  - Audio / video sync > Calibrate audio: tap along to the clicks.
    Then Calibrate video: tap as the notes cross the line.
    Do the audio one first.

B goes back in every menu. On the song list: A picks a song, then choose the instrument and
difficulty. X deletes a song, ZR changes the sort order, and left/right
jump between letters.

Hold minus for two seconds on any screen to get back to the song list.
Settings are saved in settings.cfg and high scores in scores.cfg, both
in sd:/switch/switch-hero/.
